NEONFLOW / METHODOLOGY

Reading the market matrix

Each fine green or red curve represents relative stock strength against the previous NSE close. The central bars show the sampled distribution of gainers and losers, while the right tiles rank a directional 0–100 composite score using completed NSE 5-minute bars (or 20D momentum / daily % change when selected).

The lines are visual relationships, not orders, execution volume or evidence of institutional trading. Futures OI, where available, comes from the nearest stock-futures contract and the OI percentage compares live samples since the app started.

Demo mode is synthetic, and no order-placement endpoints are enabled. Keep your Kite API credentials only in the backend .env file.
